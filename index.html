Your ads × your flow

Three promises in your videos. One paywall for all of them.

The Meta Ad Library shows 32 ads from your “Hevy” page, in 60 versions, from 3 Nov 2025 to 23 Mar 2026; none is running on 16 November. The text mostly says “track it”; the videos carry three different promises: seeing progress, a log that remembers your weights, and “the gym’s Strava”. Every install then meets the same sign-up screen, the same 12 questions and “Get Full Access to Hevy”.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by the campaign an install came from, so the first screens repeat what the video showed, and each video is judged on the subscriptions it brings, not the installs. With no ads live, the same split works on the answer every install already gives you: build my own, or be guided.
